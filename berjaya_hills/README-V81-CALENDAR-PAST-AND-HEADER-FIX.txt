Berjaya Hills v81 — Calendar Past Classification & Header Alignment

- Public calendar now classifies an event as past when its end date is before the current local calendar date, rather than comparing exact time-of-day. This ensures an event dated 30 Sep 2026 is shown as past on 3 Oct 2026 even if its stored end time is later in the day/timezone.
- Upcoming means the event start date is after today; events occurring today are treated as ongoing/today, not upcoming.
- Past/upcoming totals and period filters use the same date-based rule.
- Explore-shell authenticated header keeps the account name and Sign out button aligned to the far right; Sign out remains in the header, not in the sidebar.
- No event records were fabricated or inserted.
